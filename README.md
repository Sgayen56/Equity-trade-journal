## What's Inside

**1. P&L Dashboard**  
High-level metrics, win/loss breakdown, profit factor, and a realized P&L chart by instrument.

**2. Trade P&L & Stories**  
For each trade: entry/exit rationale, thesis, risk lesson, and formula-driven return calculations.

**3. Risk & Controls**  
Post-trade reflections, sizing mistakes (looking at you, IRFC breakout), and data-quality checks.

I wanted to move beyond "I trade stocks" and actually measure what works, what doesn't, and why. This workbook connects market interest with disciplined P&L analysis — and gives me a concrete story to walk through in interviews.
